Emergency

Police, ambulance and mountain rescue all start here. It works on any mobile network, and you can also try 112.

Mountain rescueCall 999, ask for Police, then ask for Mountain Rescue.

Who to call

Group chat

Everyone’s phone numbers are in the WhatsApp group chat. Mobile signal around the campsite is patchy, so save the campsite number and share plans in the chat before you set off.

If someone is hurt or missing on the mountain

  • Stay together, and tell the group before anyone splits off.
  • Call 999 and give your location, what has happened, how many people are involved and your phone number.
  • Stay where you are, keep warm and keep your phone on.
  • With no signal, signal for help with six whistle blasts or torch flashes in a minute, then wait a minute and repeat.
